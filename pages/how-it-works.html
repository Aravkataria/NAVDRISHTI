Standard Micro-Contracts (IP Rights)

Under Indian Intellectual Property Law, creators naturally own their work unless assigned in writing. SkillBridge AI enforces a mandatory 1-click IP transfer clause in every application, ensuring business clients own 100% of commercial usage rights upon milestone payout.

TDS & GST Compliance (Indian Tax Norms)

Micro-freelance gigs below ₹30,000 are TDS-exempt under Section 194J/194C thresholds. For platform commission, SkillBridge AI generates GSTIN-compliant tax invoices charging 18% GST on the 10% platform fee, allowing registered local shops to claim input tax credits.

College Verification Safeguards

To prevent marketplace abuse and ensure students only compete with peers, SkillBridge validates student enrollment using AI OCR on university ID cards and fee receipts.
