# SkillBridge AI — Legal & Indian Regulatory Compliance Guide

## 1. Student Age & College Verification
- **Indian Contract Act (1872):** Minors (under 18) cannot enter into legally enforceable commercial contracts.
- **SkillBridge Policy:** All registered student partners must be active college students enrolled in an accredited higher education institution.
- **Verification System:** AI OCR scans student ID cards and semester fee slips to extract roll numbers and active batch years.

---

## 2. Intellectual Property (IP) & Commercial Rights Transfer
- **The Law:** Under the Indian Copyright Act (1957), copyright initially resides with the creator (the student designer or developer) unless assigned in writing.
- **The Micro-Contract:** Every project application enforces a legally binding digital assignment clause:
  > *"I agree that all intellectual property (IP), source files, and commercial usage rights transfer entirely to the business client upon milestone approval and payment release."*
- **Protection for Merchants:** Businesses receive full commercial indemnification and clean ownership of all graphics, code, and videos upon escrow release.

---

## 3. Taxation: TDS & GST Framework
- **Tax Deducted at Source (TDS):**
  - Section 194J/194C of the Indian Income Tax Act mandates TDS deductions only when annual payments to a single professional exceed ₹30,000.
  - Micro-gigs on SkillBridge AI (₹500–₹2,000) fall well below this statutory limit, removing tax withholding friction for student participants.
- **Goods and Services Tax (GST):**
  - Marketplace commissions are subject to 18% GST (SAC code 998313).
  - On a ₹500 project, SkillBridge AI generates a GSTIN-compliant tax invoice:
    - Gross Project Value: ₹500.00
    - Student Partner Net Payout: ₹450.00
    - Platform Commission (Net): ₹42.37
    - CGST (9%) + SGST (9%): ₹7.63
    - Total Platform Take: ₹50.00 (Inclusive of GST)
