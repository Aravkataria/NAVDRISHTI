# SkillBridge AI — 3-Minute Hackathon Pitch Script & Judge Q&A

## ⏱️ Pitch Timeline (180 Seconds)

### 0:00 – 0:35 | The Hook & The Problem
> *"Every year, millions of Indian college students learn Canva, video editing, and web development. But when they try to monetize, they hit a brutal Catch-22: Global freelance platforms like Upwork and Fiverr won't hire them because they don't have reviews, but they can't get reviews without gigs.*
>
> *Meanwhile, our neighborhood businesses—bakeries, gyms, salons, coaching classes—urgently need digital flyers and Instagram reels, but they cannot afford 20,000-rupee agency retainers or navigate international platforms."*

---

### 0:35 – 1:15 | The Solution (Show Front Page & AI Matching)
> *"Enter **SkillBridge AI**. We connect verified college students with local businesses for paid micro-projects between ₹500 and ₹2,000. 
> 
> Look at our live platform: In 1 click, our AI Matching Engine scans student skills against local business demand, calculating a deterministic 96% Match score based on skill tags, budget compatibility, and student weekly availability."*

---

### 1:15 – 1:55 | Meet VAJRA AI Assistant
> *"Students often struggle with writing professional proposals. Notice our built-in SLM assistant, **VAJRA**. With 1 click, Priya can ask VAJRA to craft a winning pitch for the bakery poster gig. VAJRA generates a polished proposal highlighting her Canva and Figma skills, and with 1 click, inserts it directly into the application modal."*

---

### 1:55 – 2:30 | The Micro-Contract & Simulated Escrow
> *"When Priya applies, she signs our standard micro-contract transferring commercial IP rights to the client. Switching to the business portal: Sweet Delights Bakery reviews Priya's submitted draft. They click **Approve & Release**, and our escrow system automatically splits the funds:
> • **₹450 (90%)** goes directly to Priya's student wallet.
> • **₹50 (10%)** is retained as SkillBridge platform revenue, instantly generating a GST-compliant tax receipt."*

---

### 2:30 – 3:00 | Business Model & Defensibility
> *"Our platform take-rate is a transparent 10%. Students stay on our platform because every completed project earns them verified college portfolio credits and experience badges that help them land full-time jobs during campus placements. SkillBridge AI turns student free hours into career capital."*

---

## 🎯 Anticipated Judge Q&A

### Q1: "Isn't this just Fiverr or Upwork?"
> **Answer:** *"No. On Fiverr and Upwork, a college student with zero reviews competes with 10-year global professionals charging hourly dollars. SkillBridge AI is built exclusively for hyper-local micro-tasks (₹500–₹2,000) where local merchants want fast, budget help and students get their first portfolio proof."*

### Q2: "Why wouldn't the bakery just pay cash directly next time (platform leakage)?"
> **Answer:** *"Students stay because every completed project earns them a College Experience Certificate and builds a verified public skill score that colleges and campus recruiters evaluate during final placements."*

### Q3: "How do you handle Indian tax compliance (GST & TDS)?"
> **Answer:** *"Micro-freelance gigs below ₹30,000 are exempt from TDS withholding under Section 194J thresholds. For our 10% platform fee, we generate automated GSTIN invoices charging 18% GST, enabling registered businesses to claim input tax credits."*
