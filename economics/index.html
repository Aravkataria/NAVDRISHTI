Standard Micro-Contracts (IP Rights)

Under Indian Intellectual Property Law, creators naturally own their work unless assigned in writing. SkillBridge enforces a mandatory 1-click IP transfer clause in every application, ensuring business clients own 100% of commercial usage rights upon milestone payout.

GST & Tax Compliance

The 10% platform commission includes 18% GST on facilitation fees. Corporate clients receive automated GST tax invoices with valid HSN/SAC codes for accounting write-offs.

DigiLocker & OCR College Proof

Students upload university enrollment cards or fee receipts. Automated OCR validates active college status, safeguarding student-exclusive work opportunities against external agency spam.
