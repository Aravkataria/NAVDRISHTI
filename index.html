FAQ.

Looking for answers? Everything you need to know about ARGUS.

ARGUS connects verified college students with neighborhood small businesses (bakeries, salons, coaching institutes, gyms, boutiques) for paid digital micro-tasks priced between ₹500 and ₹2,500. Students earn fair pay while building a commercial portfolio, and merchants get affordable digital marketing without expensive agency retainers.
When a business posts a project, the total budget is pre-funded into an escrow vault. The student is guaranteed that payment is ready upon completion. The client inspects the submitted draft before releasing the funds with one click. If revisions are required, the student updates the work until satisfaction.
This price tier aligns perfectly with discrete, well-scoped micro-tasks (3 festive social media posters, a 30-second edited reel, a 1-page responsive landing page, or Google Business profile setup). It makes services accessible to small business budgets while providing college students meaningful earnings for 3–8 hours of work.
Students upload their college ID card or university enrollment proof in the Student Hub. Our automated verification extracts university name, roll number, and valid semester dates to grant a verified student badge. This builds trust with clients and prioritizes the student in project matching.
Every project includes the standard ARGUS Micro-Contract clause: all commercial copyrights, source files, and marketing usage rights transfer automatically and permanently to the business client upon milestone approval and payment release.
ARGUS charges a transparent 10% platform facilitation fee. For a ₹1,000 project, ₹900 goes directly into the student freelancer's wallet, and ₹100 covers platform matching, verification, and hosting. No hidden withdrawal fees or currency conversion deductions.
